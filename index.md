# Formy — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 13 October 2026**

Formy ("the app", package `io.github.coro19.formy`) is a fitness app that counts repetitions and checks exercise form using the phone's camera, and lets you log workouts by hand. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and your rights.

## Who is responsible

The app is developed by Vlad Coroban, an individual developer based in Timișoara, Romania, who is the data controller for the processing described here.
Contact: **corobanvlad10@gmail.com**

## Summary

- Camera images are analysed on your phone and never saved or uploaded.
- Your workout history stays on your phone. The developer never receives it.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show ads, as described below, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland asks for your consent first.
- There are no accounts, no analytics and no data sales.

## Camera

The camera permission is used only while you track a set with the camera. Each frame is analysed on the device to locate body joints, then discarded. The app does not record, store or upload photos or video, and no camera data leaves your phone. You can log workouts by hand without granting the camera permission, and you can revoke it at any time in Android settings.

## Workout data and settings

The app stores the following on your device, in its private storage:

- your workout history: exercises, dates and times, sets, reps, weights, durations, and, for camera-tracked sets, form scores and form cues
- exercises you create yourself
- your app settings, such as the weight unit, default camera and keep-screen-on

This data is not sent to the developer or to any third party. It is kept until you delete it. You can delete individual sets or whole workouts in History. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it from the device.

**Device backup.** If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this app data in your device backup stored in your own Google account, and restore it if you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. This backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings. The developer has no access to it. You can turn backup off, or delete backups, in your Android and Google account settings.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited (in the EEA) and Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

- device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
- your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
- product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views
- diagnostic information, such as app and SDK performance

**Consent.** If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent (legal basis: consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, as described in Google's policies. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**, which is shown in regions where it is required. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

Google processes this data as an independent controller under its own policies:

- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on the device), and manage ad personalisation at [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com).

**International transfers.** Google may process this data outside the EEA, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for such transfers.

## What the app does not collect

The app has no accounts and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, video or precise location. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs besides what is built into Google AdMob. No data is sold.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time.

- **Workout data and settings** stay on your device and are fully under your control: you can view, edit and delete them in the app.
- **Advertising data** is held by Google. Use the consent choice in Settings → Privacy options, your Android ad settings, and the Google tools linked above. You can also contact the developer, who will help you where he can.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Romania, this is the *Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal* (ANSPDCP), [www.dataprotection.ro](https://www.dataprotection.ro). You can also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live.

## Security

Data on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent to Google by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and the developer does not knowingly process data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact the developer.
